# Privacy Policy for Motion Game Controller

Motion Game Controller requires access to your device's camera to track physical body movements and translate them into keyboard inputs for web games.

**Data Processing & Storage**
All motion tracking and video processing are performed locally on your device using MediaPipe. Video feeds, biometric data, and physical motion data are processed strictly on-device and never leave your computer.

**Data Collection**
We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information, video data, audio data, or browsing history to external servers. Your custom key configurations and sensitivity settings are saved locally in your browser using Chrome's storage API.

**Third-Party Sharing**
We do not sell, trade, or transfer any user data to outside parties.
